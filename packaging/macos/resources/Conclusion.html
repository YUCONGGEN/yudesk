YuDesk is installed

Open Applications → YuDesk. If an earlier copy is still running, finish your session and quit it before opening the installed version.

macOS may request permission for features such as screen recording, accessibility or local networking. Review these requests in System Settings when you use the feature.

Screen capture needs Screen Recording permission. Receiving keyboard/mouse input additionally needs the separately provisioned cliclick tool and Accessibility permission. Your administrator must verify that YuDesk launched from Finder can locate that tool.

To remove YuDesk, quit the app and move it from Applications to the Trash. Your saved device identity and preferences are retained in your user account.
